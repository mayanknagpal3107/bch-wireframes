Our values

What we stand by

From the live company story and the brand: engineering depth, trust, progressive craft, glocal reach. har haal mein.

Engineering depth

1965 joint venture with Cutler-Hammer, USA. Wholly Indian since 1977. Two plants — Faridabad and Rudrapur — one quality standard.

Proven to perform

“Built to withstand. Proven to perform.” Ruggedness, robustness, reliability — type-tested ranges to IEC / IS, including ISO 9001, 14001 and 45001.

Stay dependable

“Designed to stay relevant. Trusted to stay dependable.” Intelligent design and continuity — products tried for Indian duty and exported.

Glocal partner

The trusted glocal switchgear house for farmers, electricians, dealers, OEMs and specifiers. Depth, discipline, progressive craft.
