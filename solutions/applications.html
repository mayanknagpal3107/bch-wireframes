Applications

Long-term IA: six application doors (not twenty SKU-use-cases). Each door is a job — products shown as ranges, never a single exact SKU.

A1

Agriculture & pump sets

Irrigation, borewell and farm-duty starting — the village pump through the plant pump house.

Products used: Shakti / Citation / submersible / oil-immersed starters · 3–20 HP · 110–415 V
 A2

Motor control & protection

Start, protect, coordinate — contactors, MPCB, OLR / EOCR on LV motors.

Products used: contactors · MPCB · OLR/EOCR · 0.1–25 A (MPCB) · 110–415 V
 A3

Power control & protection

Incomer and distribution trees — ACB, MCCB, MCB, RCCB, SDF.

Products used: ACB · MCCB · MCB · RCCB · SDF · 16–630 A (MCCB) · 110–415 V
 A4

Detection & sensing

End-of-travel, crane and conveyor position — limit switches, not a sensor catalogue dump.

Products used: limit switches · 110–415 V control circuits
 A5

Heavy machinery & crane

Duty panels for mills, hoists and cranes — engineered, not an HP chip.

Products used: crane panels · mill-duty contactors · Form 2–4B · kA / IP ranges
 A6

Panel systems

MCC / PCC / IMCC / APFC — enquire for a board, not a catalogue SKU.

Products used: engineered panels · Form 2–4B · kA range · IP range
