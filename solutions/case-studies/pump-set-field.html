Duty

Agriculture and pump-set starting: irrigation, borewell and agri motor applications where a DOL or pump starter is commonly specified. See Agriculture and motor starters.

Challenge

Direct-on-line starting applies full line voltage. Starting current is high compared with running current. Field sites add frequent starts, voltage fluctuation, dust or moisture, and enclosure access. Selection should review motor full-load current, coil voltage, overload range, starts per hour and site conditions — the same checks as the live DOL article.
