1. Challenge

Direct-on-line starts, frequent cycling, and dusty field enclosures were causing nuisance trips and downtime on continuous pump duty. The plant needed a starter range that holds up under Indian voltage and ambient conditions — without guessing ratings from a brochure.

2. Who / where used

Citation starters on continuous pump-set duty at a mid-size process OEM. Site: industrial campus with irrigation and process water loops.

Specified Citation starters for the pump duty: motor FLC, coil voltage, overload band, and starts-per-hour reviewed with the dealer / application engineer. Type-tested range supplied through the stocking channel — no invented SKU pricing on the site.
