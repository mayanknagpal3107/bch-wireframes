1. Challenge

A production line stopped on a failed control device. Waiting for a courier spare meant hours of lost output. Maintenance needed an engineer on site — with the right part — the same shift.

2. Who / where used

Maintenance team at a manufacturing site in Pune. Photo and location callout for the wireframe; customer name approved for field use.

Branch stock plus a resident engineer reached the line within four hours. Device identified, swapped, and the line restarted — without inventing a toll-free SLA on this page. Use Customer care and Sales offices for live channels.
