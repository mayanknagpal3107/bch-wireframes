What is BCH business?

BCH has strong presence in Industrial Products. The Product verticals are:

  • Motor Starters and Submersible Pump Starters
  • Switchgear & Control Gears
  • Power Controls
  • Industrial Enclosures
  • Brake & Crane control
  • Reactive Power management
  • LV Panels
  • Automation
  • Wires & Cables
Where are the factories and branch offices located in India?

BCH has set up 2 manufacturing plants located at Faridabad & Rudrapur. 30 Branch offices across India.

Internationally BCH has presence through its channel partners
