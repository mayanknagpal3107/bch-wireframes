Notify BCH about a counterfeit product

Serious channel. If you have found, been offered, or purchased a product that may not be genuine BCH — tell us where, from whom, and what it is. We investigate and act on the source.

How we take action
01 · Report

You notify us

Where found, where purchased (dealer / city), product name, photo of nameplate or packing.

02 · Source

We log the channel

Invoice, seller, location — so the report is not anonymous rumour.

03 · Investigation

Product & channel

Range authenticity, packing, documents. Sales office and channel team are looped in.

04 · Action

Channel action

Follow-up with the source, dealer action where warranted, and a reply to you.
