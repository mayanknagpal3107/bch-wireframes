Verify your product

Check the unit you bought is original BCH. Marks below are wireframe placeholders — BCH will confirm hologram / QR / serial / laser practice before production.

How to check
01 · Hologram

Packing hologram

Placeholder. Look for a BCH hologram on the carton or bag — production will confirm the real mark.

02 · QR

QR on nameplate

Placeholder. Scan the QR on the nameplate or sticker if present, then enter the code here.

03 · Serial

Serial / batch

Placeholder. Read the serial or batch on the nameplate. Type it in the form.

04 · Laser

Laser mark

Placeholder. Some ranges carry a laser-etched mark on the body or nameplate. Photograph it if unsure.
