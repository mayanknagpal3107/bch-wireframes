R&D & quality

R&D and quality assurance

BCH is known for its R&D, Quality Assurance in superior product design, manufacturing and performance for over 5 decades. All BCH products conform to latest national and international standards.

BCH has invested in state of the art research and development facilities including in house laboratories for electrical & material testing equipment. BCH prides itself on its products quality which is ensured by the full-fledged Quality Assurance department.

Testing facilities

Testing lab is equipped with the latest testing equipment including various current sources capable of dealing with high current requirements, voltage sources, transformers etc.

Electrical & material tests

The lab is capable of conducting magnetic tests, temperature rise tests, endurance tests, dielectric strength and leakage current tests etc.

IP / environment

We can test Equipment for up to IP65 with our dust chamber. Our environmental chamber allows us to test newly developed products within temperature ranges from -25°C to +70°C.
