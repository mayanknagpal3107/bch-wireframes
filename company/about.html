Our values

Identity that carries the legacy: engineering depth, trust, progressive. Har haal mein.

Engineering depth

Founded 1965 as a Cutler-Hammer joint venture. Independent Indian house since 1977. Faridabad (15 acres) and Rudrapur (12 acres, 2010).

Trust

“Built to withstand. Proven to perform.” ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2015, ISO 45001:2018. Products conform to national and international standards.

Progressive

“Designed to stay relevant. Trusted to stay dependable.” In-house R&D, type-test labs, products developed for Indian markets and export.
