Privacy Policy

How BCH Electric Limited handles personal data on this wireframe. Includes the DPDP section below.

Wireframe — copy to confirm. One privacy notice for this preview. Grievance: it_support@bchindia.com. Not counsel-approved.

What we collect

Business contact details you submit on enquiry, dealer, support, or career forms: name, company, email, phone, role, and the product or support topic you describe. We may also receive technical data such as IP address and browser type for site operation.

How we use it

To respond to your request, route a datasheet or dealer introduction, support a product question, or consider a career application. We do not sell personal data. We do not publish SKU prices on this site.

Cookies

This site may use cookies for language/region preference and basic analytics. Cookies are not used to sell personal data.

Digital Personal Data Protection Act (India)

Under India’s DPDP Act, 2023 we aim for a lawful purpose, notice to the Data Principal, purpose limitation, data minimisation, reasonable security, and a path to correct or erase data — plus a grievance channel.

  • Enquiry fields exist to fulfil that request — Enquire, Datasheet, Dealer, Support, Complaint, or Other.
  • We do not reuse that data for unrelated marketing without a further, clear purpose.
  • Records are kept only as long as needed to complete the request or meet a legal obligation.

Grievance

For access, correction, erasure, or a complaint about data handling, write to it_support@bchindia.com. Wireframe — copy to confirm.
