Digital Personal Data Protection Act

Wireframe policy narrative for how this site, enquiries and research use personal data under India’s DPDP Act. Not a fake certification. Grievance: it_support@bchindia.com (from the live data-privacy page).

What DPDP is

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP) is India’s law on processing digital personal data. It requires a lawful purpose, notice to the Data Principal, purpose limitation, data minimisation, reasonable security, and a path to correct or erase data — plus a grievance channel.

BCH Electric Limited processes personal data when you use bchindia.com, submit an enquiry, download a document, apply for a role, or take part in product research. This page is the wireframe of that narrative. The live privacy statement remains on Data privacy.

How this site complies

Lawful basis

We collect what you give us to respond to an enquiry, send a datasheet, locate a dealer, support a product, or consider a career application. We do not sell personal data. Business contact details on an enquiry form are processed to fulfil that request.

Purpose limitation

Enquiry fields (name, business email, company, role, country, what you are looking for) exist to route the request — Enquire, Datasheet, Dealer, Support, or Other. We do not reuse that data for unrelated marketing without a further, clear purpose.

Retention

Contact and enquiry records are kept only as long as needed to complete the request, keep a support history, or meet a legal/accounting obligation — then deleted or archived. Attachment uploads on the wireframe are placeholders; production would apply the same retention rules.

Research

If we invite you to a product or site-research interview, participation is voluntary. Notes are used to improve the catalogue and the website, not to profile you for advertising. You may decline or withdraw.

Grievance

For access, correction, erasure, or a complaint about how we handle personal data, write to it_support@bchindia.com. We will use reasonable efforts to respond promptly. This is the same grievance address published on the live data-privacy policy.

Wireframe only — this is not a certificate, audit mark, or DPDP “compliance badge.” Production legal text is owned by BCH and counsel.
